`POST /kling/elements` and `POST /kling/voices` manage platform assets through `action`: `list` lists your own assets, `presets` retrieves preset assets, `retrieve` queries an `id`, and `delete` deletes your own `id`. Preset assets cannot be deleted. All references use the platform IDs returned by the API; assets are isolated by user and application. Management operations are not billed.

`list` and `presets` support `page_num` (1–1000, default 1) and `page_size` (1–500, default 30). Preset assets can be used for generation after they are read for the first time. Deleted assets can no longer be used for new tasks.

## Create a Voice

Submit `action="create"`, a `voice_name` of up to 20 characters, and `voice_url` to `/kling/voices`. Audio should be a clear single-person voice of 5–30 seconds, supporting mp3, wav, mp4, and mov. Each creation is charged 0.07 Credits; failed tasks follow the platform's failed-task billing rules. When `async=true` or `callback_url` is provided, `task_id` is returned first, and the final result is queried through `/kling/tasks`.

```json
{
  "action": "create",
  "voice_name": "Narrator",
  "voice_url": "https://cdn.acedata.cloud/assets/examples/fish/5ade0339-5f11-487e-aacc-06a908271706-8e3fcb0e5547.mp3",
  "async": true
}
```

After successful creation, use `voice_list=[{"voice_id":"platform voice ID"}]` in `/kling/videos`. Supports 1–2 voices, limited to `model="kling-v2-6"`, `mode="pro"`, and `generate_audio=true`. Use `&lt;&lt;<voice_1>>>` or `&lt;&lt;<voice_2>>>` in `prompt` to specify the selected voice. Videos with specified voices are billed at 1.68 Credits/second based on actual output duration, supporting fractional seconds; `usage.duration` in the result is the actual metered duration. For example, an output of 5.04 seconds corresponds to 8.4672 Credits. Cannot be used together with `element_list`. Returns 502 when the service does not provide a valid output duration.

The estimated total price before generation is calculated based on the requested duration: 5 seconds costs 8.4 Credits, and 10 seconds costs 16.8 Credits; the final bill is settled based on the actual output duration.

## Subject References

Use `element_list=[{"element_id":"platform subject ID"}]` in `/kling/videos` to reference retrieved preset subjects. Supports 1–3 subjects, limited to V3, V3 Omni, and O1. The total number of subjects and images for Omni/O1 is at most 7; with a reference video, at most 4. Other generation limitations still apply.

Custom subject creation is not yet available; `action="create"` returns 503; creation tasks will not be submitted before price confirmation. The request contract under preparation includes `element_name` (up to 20 characters), `element_description` (up to 100 characters), and `reference_type`. `image_refer` uses a front-facing image and 1–3 reference images; `video_refer` uses one reference video of 3–8 seconds, 1080p, and up to 200 MB. The two material formats cannot be mixed. This preparation contract does not indicate that creation capability has been launched.
