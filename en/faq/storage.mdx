## How long do result URLs live?

| Asset | Retention |
|---|---|
| Midjourney / Flux / Seedream image URLs | **30 days** |
| Sora / Veo / Kling / Hailuo / Luma video URLs | **30 days** |
| Suno / Producer music URLs | **90 days** |
| Fish Audio temp TTS URLs | **24 hours** |

<Note>
  **Don't treat our URLs as permanent storage.** In production, copy assets to your own object storage (S3 / OSS / COS / R2) as soon as you receive them.
</Note>

## How fast should I download?

Downloads are **free and don't consume quota**. Recommended:

- Trigger an async download right after `success` / webhook
- Retry 3× with exponential backoff (2 s, 4 s, 8 s)
- Store your own URL; treat ours as backup

## Uploading files to us

Many endpoints accept a reference image / video. **Recommended**: host on your own storage and pass a URL.

Requirements:

- HTTPS (some upstreams reject HTTP)
- Reachable for the duration of the request (long-term ideally)
- Image ≤ 25 MB, video reference ≤ 200 MB
- No strict Referer / hotlink protection

## No storage of your own?

Use our temporary upload endpoint (OAuth-authenticated):

```bash
POST https://api.acedata.cloud/files/upload
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN
Content-Type: multipart/form-data

file=@/path/to/local.png
```

Response:

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "url": "https://cdn.acedata.cloud/uploads/.../local.png",
  "expires_at": "2026-01-15T10:30:00Z"
}
```

Limits: upload ≤ 25 MB, URL valid 7 days, only consumable by your own subsequent calls.

## COS Acceleration / Global Distribution

- CDN is a mix of EdgeOne and other providers, globally distributed
- Pulls from the closest node by default; **first hit may cold-start ~500 ms**
- For critical hot assets, fan out to your own cache origin

## Moderation & Retention

- We **don't** retain prompts, reference inputs, or generated outputs long-term
- Compliance requires us to retain **30 days** of access metadata
- Content blocked by moderation is **immediately discarded** — nothing kept

## Deleting your content

To purge a specific task:

1. Find the `task_id`
2. Email `privacy@acedata.cloud` with the list
3. Processed within 24 hours typically
