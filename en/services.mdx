Choose a service below. Start with [Quickstart](/en/quickstart) to obtain an API key and make your first request. Each guide describes the inputs, supported models and output format for that service.

For **GPT Image 2.6**, choose [Flare](https://platform.acedata.cloud/models/gpt-image-2.6-flare?m=image&lang=en) or [Sunburst](https://platform.acedata.cloud/models/gpt-image-2.6-sunburst?m=image&lang=en), then follow [image generation](/en/guides/openai/openai_images_generations) or [image editing](/en/guides/openai/openai_images_edits).
