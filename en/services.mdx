Choose a service below. Start with [Quickstart](https://platform.acedata.cloud/documents/api-quickstart?lang=en) to obtain an API key and make your first request. Each guide describes the inputs, supported models and output format for that service.

For **GPT Image 2.6**, choose [Flare](https://platform.acedata.cloud/models/gpt-image-2.6-flare?m=image&lang=en) or [Sunburst](https://platform.acedata.cloud/models/gpt-image-2.6-sunburst?m=image&lang=en), then follow [image generation](https://platform.acedata.cloud/documents/openai-images-generations-integration?lang=en) or [image editing](https://platform.acedata.cloud/documents/openai-images-edits-integration?lang=en).
