After holding $ACE and verifying your Solana wallet, you can receive call discounts on Ace Data Cloud **Credits API services that support this benefit**. $ACE always remains in your wallet; the platform only reads the on-chain balance and will not custody, lock, transfer, or consume tokens.

> $ACE is not a platform top-up balance, nor is it a payment authorization or staking product. Discounts apply only to the Credits API services listed on the page, and do not apply to top-up orders, x402 USDC payments, or other non-Credits consumption paths.

## Step 1: Purchase $ACE

Purchasing $ACE **does not require binding a wallet on the platform first**. You only need to prepare:

- A Solana mainnet wallet, such as Phantom or Solflare;
- SOL, USDC, or other assets actually supported by the page for swapping;
- A small amount of SOL to pay Solana network fees.

The platform will not ask you to provide your seed phrase or private key. Any page asking you to submit a seed phrase, private key, or remotely control your wallet is not part of the normal process.

Open the [$ACE Holding Benefits Page](https://platform.acedata.cloud/console/coin), verify the complete Mint in the first-step card, and click “Purchase $ACE”; you can also open directly:

### 2. Connect Your Solana Wallet

Connect the prepared wallet on the swap page, and confirm that the current network is the Solana mainnet. When using a wallet for the first time, first understand the wallet’s own security notices and transaction confirmation interface.

### 3. Select the Asset and Amount to Swap

Select SOL, USDC, or other assets actually supported by the page as the payment asset, and enter the amount you wish to swap. Confirm that the target token Mint shown on the page exactly matches the following address:

Before submitting, check:

### 5. Confirm in Your Wallet

After confirmation, wait for the Solana network to complete the transaction, then return to your wallet to check the $ACE balance. Do not close or refresh a pop-up that is still waiting for wallet confirmation, unless the wallet clearly indicates that the transaction has failed or been canceled.

## Step 2: Connect and Verify Your Wallet After Purchase

After the purchase is complete, return to the platform to connect and verify your wallet. Binding is not required before purchase; the purchase itself also does not automatically bind an account. Please use **the same wallet that holds $ACE** to complete the following steps:

1. Return to the [$ACE Holding Benefits Page](https://platform.acedata.cloud/console/coin);
2. Click “Connect Wallet” and select the wallet you just used;
3. Click “Verify and Bind”;
4. Sign the ownership verification message displayed by the platform in your wallet;
5. Wait for the platform to synchronize the finalized on-chain balance.

The ownership verification signature only proves that you control the wallet, and **does not authorize payment or token transfers**. The normal process involves signing a readable message and will not require you to transfer funds to an unknown address.

After successful verification, the page will display the abbreviated wallet address, $ACE balance, last synchronization time, and currently applicable discount. There may be a delay between the completion of the on-chain transaction and the platform’s balance synchronization, so the discount may not appear immediately after purchase.

Balances are read by background tasks from the Solana finalized state. Coin-type discounts have a validity period:

- After successful synchronization, discounts that meet the thresholds will be applied automatically;
- When temporary synchronization fails, the page will indicate an abnormal balance status;
- If the balance still cannot be refreshed after the validity period expires, the billing path will suspend stale discounts;
- After the wallet balance decreases or $ACE is sold, the next successful synchronization will recalculate benefits based on the new balance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my platform balance still 0 after purchasing?

First confirm that the transaction has been completed on the Solana mainnet, and confirm that the same wallet holding $ACE is bound. The platform reads the finalized on-chain state, and transaction confirmation and backend synchronization may both cause brief delays. If the page continues to indicate a synchronization error, please try again later or contact the support team.

### Why am I unable to sign the verification message?

The current preferred process requires the wallet to support `signMessage`. Please update your wallet version, or use a Solana wallet that supports message signing. Do not bypass verification through transfers or by submitting your private key.

### What should I do if I connected the wrong wallet?

“Disconnect Wallet” in the browser only disconnects the current browser connection and does not change the platform binding. To replace the benefits wallet, click “Unbind Wallet” on the holdings benefits page, confirm the revocation of the current ACE discount, and then connect and verify the new wallet.

### Can a wallet signature transfer my assets?

No. The binding process signs an ownership verification message, not a token transfer or payment authorization. You should still verify the message content displayed by the wallet before signing; if a transaction, transfer, or unfamiliar authorization request appears, reject it.

### How much $ACE do I need to purchase to receive a discount?

The current first-tier threshold is 100,000 $ACE, which provides a 1% discount on supported Credits API services. See the table above for each tier; the actually applicable services and current discounts are subject to the holdings benefits page.

### Which services support $ACE benefits?

Please check the “Services Supporting ACE Benefits” section on the holdings benefits page. Top-up orders, x402 USDC payments, and non-Credits consumption paths do not use this discount.

### What happens after selling or transferring out $ACE?

Benefits are calculated based on the on-chain balance. After the balance decreases, the next successful synchronization will reduce or revoke the corresponding discount; stale discounts also have an expiration period and will not be retained indefinitely.

### What should I do if I cannot access my old wallet?

Log in to your platform account, click “Unbind Wallet” on the holdings benefits page, and confirm. Unbinding only revokes the wallet association and ACE discount on the platform; it does not transfer, destroy, or operate any assets in the wallet. After completion, you can connect and verify a new wallet.
